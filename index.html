See how slides and spreadsheets become chunks.

Most RAG pipelines flatten Office files into a wall of text and lose what made them useful: headers, merged cells, chart data, speaker notes. This one keeps the structure, shows you exactly what it extracted, and answers with citations to the slide or row.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.
